TetoTV self-built native playback notice
========================================

This Android application includes libmpv.so and
libmediakitandroidhelper.so compiled by TetoTV from immutable upstream source
revisions. Release builds do not download or fall back to the upstream
prebuilt media-kit native JARs.

The linked playback source includes:

- mpv 78d43740f52db817d98bcf24fb30a76ab6fa13ff, built with gpl=false
  (LGPL-2.1-or-later source terms; the effective combined build is treated
  conservatively as LGPL-3.0-or-later).
- FFmpeg ea3d24bbe3c58b171e55fe2151fc7ffaca3ab3d2, configured with
  --disable-gpl, --disable-nonfree, and --enable-version3.
- Mbed TLS 1873d3bfc2da771672bd8e7e8f41f57e0af77f33 under its Apache-2.0

The exact component notices and applicable full license texts are bundled in
the application's open-source license screen. The libmpv link also consumes
Android NDK r25c's static zlib, libatomic, and compiler-runtime pieces; the
helper statically consumes the NDK C++ runtime, C++ ABI, unwind, libatomic, and
compiler-runtime pieces. The complete r25c runtime and toolchain notices are
therefore bundled as part of this distribution.

Exact source revisions, verified toolchain archives, patch hashes, build
instructions, output hashes, and APK replacement/relinking instructions are
recorded in docs/NATIVE_PLAYBACK_REDISTRIBUTION.md and
tool/release/native_playback_manifest.json. The source/rebuild ZIP generated
by tool/release/verify_native_redistribution.ps1 is published beside the
corresponding APK and checksum manifest.
