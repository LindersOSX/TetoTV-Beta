TetoTV direct-torrent native component provenance
==================================================

The Android direct-torrent engine resolves these Maven Central artifacts at
version 2.1.0-38:

- org.libtorrent4j:libtorrent4j
- org.libtorrent4j:libtorrent4j-android-arm
- org.libtorrent4j:libtorrent4j-android-arm64

The libtorrent4j tag v2.1.0-38 resolves to commit
09ffd391d4ef12e668cc032bffcbab47d9e2d5cb. Its libtorrent-rasterbar
submodule resolves to a01469c8d1f88dd83bed458ffccffab2727b9d2a.

The native binary also statically incorporates WebTorrent support from
libdatachannel commit 6ab310b5887eab78cf0c0767a8ced2ebff8c7479 and its
following pinned dependencies:

- libjuice 2de35247f0b15fa385406f3e2020d0e3d4d5cfcc
- usrsctp ebb18adac6501bad4501b1f6dccb67a1c85cc299
- libsrtp a566a9cfcd619e8327784aa7cff4a1276dc1e895
- plog e21baecd4753f14da64ede979c5a19302618b752

The upstream Android build uses Boost 1.89.0 and OpenSSL 3.5.2. Exact license
texts for every component named above are bundled beside this notice.
